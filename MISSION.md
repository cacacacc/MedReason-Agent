# Mission: MedReason-Agent

## Why

The user wants to build a rigorous AI research portfolio project for Canadian AI / CS / Data Science graduate applications, undergraduate research presentation, and GitHub portfolio review. The project should show the ability to design, implement, evaluate, and explain a multimodal medical reasoning agent system.

## Success looks like

- Explain why medical multimodal reasoning needs more than direct VLM answers.
- Implement a controlled experiment chain from direct VLM to supervisor-guided multi-agent reasoning.
- Produce structured experiment logs, result tables, ablation studies, and error analysis.
- Write a research-style report with clear research questions, methods, experiments, limitations, and future work.
- Answer graduate interview questions about VLMs, Medical VQA, CoT, RAG, agents, supervisor routing, verification, calibration, and evaluation.

## Constraints

- Beginner in AI Agents and Multimodal LLM implementation.
- Limited compute; avoid training large VLMs from scratch.
- Main experiments should use frozen backbones and deterministic inference settings.
- Teaching should proceed one concept at a time with understanding checks.

## Out of scope

- Building a real clinical diagnosis product.
- Large-scale VLM pretraining.
- Large-scale RLHF, reward model training, GRPO, or DAPO.
- Claiming benchmark predictions are clinical medical advice.
